01What the architecture has to deliver

Two goals, and each one splits in half

Represent cognitive structures that are recurrent and reusable in understanding and reasoning, including concept representations. And be trainable to recognize, classify and represent new events and situations.

The argument for a two-part architecture begins before either source tradition is consulted. It comes from the goals. Each of them, read carefully, asks for two things that no single mechanism maximizes at once.

02Why one system cannot do it

The pressures on the code are opposite

To keep two episodes from blurring, their codes must be separated — sparse, overlapping as little as possible. To form a category from many instances, their codes must be converged — many inputs driving one shared response. These are not two settings of one dial with a happy middle between them. They are contradictory demands on the same population.

Three further arguments

ArgumentConsequence
New learning destroys old structure A single converging system that also stores episodes must adjust the same weights that hold its categories. The abstraction degrades in proportion to how much specific experience the system has — exactly backwards.
The two generalizations differ Structural generalization reuses a skeleton with new content and generalizes across situations. Taxonomic generalization converges instances onto a shared response and generalizes across members. Neither produces the other; adding depth to a convergence hierarchy does not make it relational.
They fail in complementary places A purely structural account treats content as an arbitrary token: no similarity, no graded membership, no prototype, no partial match. A purely convergent account has no composition: it can say A and B are present, not that A stands in a relation to B. These are the two classical criticisms — systematicity and addressable grounding — and the architecture bets they are answered by different structures rather than by one better one.

03The architecture

Three structures: two abstract slowly, one binds fast

Two of the three converge slowly over different data. The third writes in one exposure and keeps its entries apart. Everything else in the architecture is about how they exchange information and what each is forbidden to do.

The Web

slow · overlapping · what things are

Converges over co-occurrence. Features feed conjunctions, conjunctions feed categories. Graded rather than criterial, bidirectional, and entered at Cardinal Nodes.

The Schema

slow · separating · how things change

Converges over transitions. A state advanced by a Relation, blind to what occupies a position — which is the only reason a skeleton transfers.

The Index

fast · sparse · what happened where

Binds one content to one Schema position and completes the pair from either half. Written after one exposure. It must never generalize.

The Web requires

Graded, not criterial — typicality and fuzzy boundaries fall out of weighted convergence rather than being added to it. Overlapping — BLACK belongs to charcoal, night, ink and cats; nothing is copied, membership is shared connectivity. Bidirectional — a live category reactivates its features, which supplies expectation, imagery, completion and production at once. Addressable — some convergence points are compact enough to be pointed at, activated and lesioned.

The Schema requires

Separation of states, or what is bound at one position contaminates another. Path consistency — two routes to one position must produce the same state, or a memory stored there becomes unreachable from a new direction. Composition — a sequence of Relations must arrive where the structure implies, including along routes never taken; this is what buys inference. And non-commutativity has to be learnable: two translations commute, father-of then brother-of need not.

04Recruitment

A binding that keeps recurring is promoted into a structure that abstracts

This is the architecture's central mechanism and the one neither source tradition supplies on its own. An Index entry written again and again is no longer a particular. It is a regularity, and it should be moved out of the fast store into a slow one. Which slow one depends on what recurred.

Recruitment versus gradient

Recruitment is the local system's substitute for credit assignment. A global gradient assigns credit by propagating error backwards to every weight that contributed. Recruitment assigns credit structurally: the unit that became active for a conjunction is the unit that owns it, and the promotion is the assignment. No error has to travel. Whether that is enough is genuinely unknown, and the honest formulation is a comparison rather than a claim — how far short of the optimized version does it fall?

05Information flow

Four paths through the same three structures

PathHow it runs
RecognitionSensory activation ascends the Web; convergence sharpens under competition; a Cardinal becomes active. If a Schema position is current, an Index entry is written or completed. Recognition is not finished when the Cardinal fires — it reactivates expected features downward, and the settled state is the recognition.
SituationRelations advance the Schema state; each sampled content is bound at the position current when it was sampled. A situation is not a list of contents. It is a set of Index entries over one Schema traversal.
InferenceCompose Relations to arrive at a position never approached this way, then query the Index there. An unvisited part of an arrangement can be reported without having been observed. This is the architecture's clearest falsifier.
ImageryActivate a Cardinal with no sensory input; it reactivates its features downward and can supply content to be bound. The same machinery runs backwards, which is why the Web must be bidirectional rather than a feedforward classifier.

Where the borrowed population terms land

Borrowed termWhere it lives here
g — structural codeThe Schema's state: position in a relational arrangement, updated by Relations.
x — content codeA Cardinal Node's activation in the Web — not a raw sensory vector.
p — conjunctive codeAn Index entry: one content bound at one Schema position.
M — associative memoryThe Index's connectivity, with its completion dynamics.

The second row is the substantive departure. In the structural tradition the content code is an arbitrary token supplied by the environment — the whole factorization argument depends on content being interchangeable. Here it is the apex of a convergence hierarchy, so it arrives with internal structure, graded similarity, prototype effects and grounding across modalities. That is what lets the architecture bind concepts into arrangements rather than tokens.

06A worked example

The letter T, and why the confusion set is the experiment

Recognizing a T, presented somewhere in a two-dimensional field, required to be recognized in a field never seen before. The discriminating power is not in the T. It is in what the T has to be told apart from.

The task traced through the structures

StructureContribution
WebRecruits the parts — oriented stroke segments, terminations, junctions.
SchemaRelations between successively sampled parts advance its state; the figure occupies a small set of positions.
IndexBinds one part to one position: this stroke here, that stroke there.
RecruitmentThe binding pattern recurs across presentations and is promoted — to both destinations at once.

That last row is the demonstration. The arrangement — a bar across the top of a stem — goes to the Schema: it is material-independent, the same whether made of cells, dots, or people standing in a field. The letter — named, pronounced, participating in words — goes to the Web as a concept with a Cardinal Node. The recruited Schema structure is then available as content in the Web, which is the loop closing on a single case. A specification must make this observable rather than merely possible: it must be possible to ask, after training, which structure a recruited unit was promoted into.

The invariances are three different problems

TransformationStatusReason
Translationmust preserve identity — free Part-relative structure carries no origin, so offsets between parts do not change when the figure moves (ADR-0004).
Scalemust preserve identity — not free Scaling multiplies every offset by a constant, so a naive relational code sees a different arrangement. Requires offsets up to a scale factor, or convergence over scales.
Rotationmust destroy identity ⊥ is T rotated 180°; ⊢ is T rotated 90°. A system invariant to rotation cannot distinguish them, because under that invariance they are the same object.
Rotation invariance is not a postponable requirement — it is a contradiction

Demanding it makes the alphabet unlearnable. This is not a quirk of the letter T: written character recognition is deliberately neither rotation- nor mirror-invariant — b/d, p/q, M/W, N/Z — and mirror invariance is the default in ventral visual processing, since a mirrored physical object is normally the same object. Literacy has to break it. So invariances belong in a specification as a signed list — which transformations must preserve identity and which must destroy it — never as a set to maximize. If a figure's identity is its arrangement, changing the arrangement must change the concept.

Three ways to pass this task while demonstrating nothing

Failure modeDefence
Memorization as generalizationTraining on every position and evaluating on those positions learns a lookup table. Positions and scales must be held out, and the specification must say which.
The local-feature shortcutAn orientation-tuned junction detector separates T from ⊥ with one local feature. The defence is scale — strokes long enough that no receptive field spans the junction and both terminations — plus evaluation at an unseen scale.
Reporting the wrong generalizationA readout that cannot separate structural transfer from taxonomic abstraction will report the first when only the second occurred. Factorial designs crossing shape against position, and shape against scale, are the minimum.

07Boundary conditions

What each structure is forbidden to do

These are the claims that make the architecture falsifiable: each predicts a specific failure if violated.

StructureMust not
WebStore particulars. Hold the arrangement of a situation. Contain a symbol, definition or property list.
SchemaKnow what occupies a position. Hold content. Ship with preloaded structures.
IndexGeneralize or merge similar entries. Persist indefinitely without promotion or decay.
Cardinal NodeContain the concept. Be assumed unique. Be a different kind of unit from any other.
Encoding boundaryCarry state across samples, or make any decision that belongs to a learning structure.

The last row is worth stating explicitly. Turning a world signal into activation is a transduction decision and nothing else. If it acquires memory of the trajectory it has quietly become the Schema, and the ability to change the encoding independently of everything downstream is lost.

08The foundation

Everything is wired. Nothing in the Web or the Schema learns.

The foundation is a baseline instrument. The three structures exist, the boundary presents multi-part figures, connectivity is sparse and declared — and no slow structure changes. Whatever selectivity appears comes from arbitrary wiring, and that number is the null a local learning rule has to beat.

One qualification, recorded as an open collision in the glossary. The Index writes a binding on every exposure, and a Hebbian association is a local rule rather than a gradient. So "no learning" means no slow structural change: nothing is recruited, nothing is promoted, no connectivity in the Web or the Schema moves. Whether the Index's one-shot write counts as learning is not settled.

Standing decisions the foundation implements

ADR-0002 — the Column is the primitive, with three private Layers. ADR-0003 — Relations are given by geometry before they are enacted by movement. ADR-0004 — structure is stored part-relative. ADR-0005 — local learning rules only. ADR-0006 — dynamics constants are declared, not derived. ADR-0007 — results are artifacts, the database is a catalogue. ADR-0008 — a Python runtime over HTTP. ADR-0009 — contrast is not the input code.

09The Column and the Space

One primitive, three private Layers

A Column is a stateful unit holding three activations, one per Layer. It carries no label and no semantic type: its function comes entirely from what it connects to. Layers are private — no Column reads another's Layers except through a declared connection. Updates are synchronous, so nothing within a tick sees a partially updated network.

An open question the glossary records rather than settles

A Cardinal Node was once said to be multimodal by definition. Splitting Space from Modality showed those are not the same requirement: colour and form are both visual, so a Column spanning them is cross-Space and single-Modality. Whether cardinality also requires crossing Modalities — and so whether the current experiment can produce a Cardinal Node at all — is not settled.

Sparsity is a specification-level constraint, not an optimization

A 64×64 field gives roughly four thousand Columns per Level, and a dense matrix between two such Levels is on the order of sixteen million weights. The foundation stores connections as triplets, so memory scales with the number of connections rather than the square of the population: wiring that would have cost 134 MB dense costs under a megabyte. But cost is the lesser reason. Convergence is only meaningful when a unit draws from a limited subset — a unit that sees everything below it is identical to every other such unit and distinguishes nothing — and separation in the Index depends on low-overlap connectivity for the same reason. A configuration in which a Column could see everything below it is refused at construction.

10A presentation

An arrangement is only visible across more than one sample

A figure with several parts cannot be presented as a single signal, because the arrangement is the thing being represented. So a presentation is a sequence. Ego visits the World positions carrying structure; at each Stop the Thalamus encodes what is there; the offset to the next Stop is the Relation. The first Stop has no Relation — one always looks from somewhere before one has moved, and inventing an offset to an origin would put a World coordinate into the structural code by the back door.

The Schema as periodic modules

A code with one unit per position is a coordinate. It separates, but composition is a lookup and nothing transfers. The foundation uses phases on a torus instead, which compose by addition, so a route never travelled arrives where the structure implies. Capacity along one axis is the least common multiple of the module periods, so a handful of small modules covers a large field — which is the whole reason an arrangement is cheaper to carry than the positions it distinguishes.

11The baseline

Four figures, 150 ticks a stop, and nothing learning

Experiment 004 asks one question: with no learning, how well does any Column separate four figures that share one feature bag? The answer is the null a local rule must beat. The measure is figure separation — the mean pairwise distance between the figures' responses, relative to the population's own magnitude.

Path consistency

error 0.0 · 144 positions

Every visited position distinct, and two routes to one position produce the same state. §3.2 holds by construction, and is now measured rather than assumed.

Completion

0.857

A partial cue settles onto a stored entry and reinstates the rest. Bindings are kept individually addressable, so "did the entries merge?" is a question the tests can actually ask.

Position variance

1.1 × 10⁻¹⁵

Machine epsilon. ADR-0004 holds at the boundary for the first time: the same figure at any origin produces the same response.

Three things the run says, and one it does not

The local-feature shortcut is real and measurable

The form Space still separates the four figures best, and it does so at its lowest Level. A 3×3 patch of a T's junction is oriented differently from a ⊥'s whatever the patch is made of. §6.5 named this trap in advance; the baseline confirms it exists. A later claim of relational learning must beat 0.148 rather than zero, and must be made at a Level where no single patch could do the work.

The colour Space is completely silent — 0 of 192 Columns

Four figures of one colour give it nothing to vary over. Honest rather than broken, but it means the confusion set exercises one Space and no more. Cardinality requires three Spaces meeting, so the current experiment cannot produce a Cardinal Node.

What removing contrast cost

The boundary used to show contrast — how much each cell differs from its neighbours — on the premise that a framework meant to record relations should be shown relations. It was also the drive magnitude every encoder was driven at. Both halves were wrong: contrast is not translation invariant, so one T at its 80 fitting origins had nine distinct signatures, and because it was the magnitude, that non-invariance reached every Space at once. The claim that CCF6 shows the network relations rather than things has therefore moved rather than gone. A Relation between two sampled positions is now the only relational quantity supplied at the boundary. Relations within a neighbourhood have to be learned — which, for a project whose next specification is a learning rule, is where they belong.

A caution about the previous baseline

The earlier run reported 0.64 selectivity for position. That was contrast, all of it. Removing it drove one factor of a variance ratio to zero, which broke the metric — the ratio then read 1.000 wherever a Column moved at all. A measurement that survives its own null is not a detail. Figure separation replaced it.

12What is still open

Predictions to be caught by, and questions a specification must settle

Five predictions

PredictionWhat would falsify it
Two kinds of conceptConcepts should divide by mechanism of individuation — featural (RED, CAT, chair) against relational (GIVE, BETWEEN, CAUSE). Not a spectrum, two mechanisms. Finding relational concepts in the Schema and object concepts in the Web would be a derived result rather than an imported assumption.
Damage dissociatesRemoving an access point should impair naming and ignition while leaving feature knowledge reachable by other routes. Damaging the Index should cost particulars, not categories; damaging the Schema, novel arrangements, not classification.
Inference precedes exposureComposition should let the system report an unvisited part of a known arrangement. The clearest single falsifier.
Promotion is visibleA unit recruited from a recurring binding should be identifiable as such, and should thereafter behave as content — bindable at a position, not merely retrievable from one.
Structural transferPerformance in a new situation sharing an old arrangement should exceed performance in one that does not, and the gap should widen with the number of arrangements experienced.

What the specification must settle

AreaOpen
StructuresWhether Web, Schema and Index are built from one kind of unit or several. What a Schema position is as activity. Index sparsity and capacity, and what happens when it is full. Whether the Web's depth is fixed or grows by recruitment.
LearningThe local rule, and what makes a co-activation "successful". What triggers recruitment — a recurrence count, a stability criterion, a competition outcome. How promotion decides between the Web and the Schema. Whether Index entries decay, and whether promotion consumes or copies them. How competition is itself learned, which both traditions leave incomplete.
InterfacesHow given and enacted Relations are kept measurably comparable. How the Schema's state is corrected when it drifts. What the encoding boundary produces, and how a change there is shown to leave everything downstream untouched. What Ego is as a mechanism.
RealizationThe fan-in rule and the sparsity of every connection class as design quantities rather than tuning parameters. The size of the field, and how that choice interacts with fan-in.
MeasurementThe unlearned baseline each claim must beat. Readouts that distinguish the two generalizations. A lesion protocol. Invariances as a signed list. Held-out positions and scales. How to determine, after training, which structure a unit was promoted into.
The first thing worth building

Two structures, two settings, or two rates? The architecture says two structures, because §2.1 argues the pressures are contradictory rather than merely different. A weaker version is worth keeping available for testing — one population with two rates and a sparsity schedule. If that turns out to work, the architecture is wrong in an interesting way, and it is cheap to find out. That makes the smallest experiment discriminating this architecture from a single-system alternative the next thing to specify.
